Turning a Website Audit into a Paid Service

By A.M. Rinas Published October 10, 2026 Category: Business 5 min read

A website audit is only valuable if it leads to actionable improvements and a paid service offering. This post documents the audit methodology, scoring system, issue prioritization, sample report structure, pricing, and how to outreach to potential clients.

Audit Methodology

The audit follows a three-step process: automated scanning, manual review, and prioritization. Automated tools include Lighthouse, W3C Validator, and Security Headers. Manual review covers UX issues, content relevance, and custom functionality testing.

Scoring & Issue Prioritization

Each identified issue is scored on impact (1-5) and effort (1-5). The priority score is impact × effort. Issues with priority ≥ 15 are classified as critical, 10-14 as high, 5-9 as medium, and 1-4 as low. This scoring helps clients understand what to fix first.

Sample Report Structure

Website Audit Report
├── Executive Summary
├── Performance Score (Lighthouse)
├── SEO Score
├── Security Score
├── Critical Issues (priority ≥ 15)
├── High Priority Issues (10-14)
├── Medium Priority Issues (5-9)
├── Low Priority Issues (1-4)
├── Recommendations
└── Next Steps


        
Pricing

        
Audit reports are priced at $299 for a comprehensive analysis. Follow-up service packages start at $99/month for ongoing monitoring and minor fixes. Discounted bundles available: audit + 3 months of maintenance for $499.


        
Outreach

        
Outreach emails should focus on the value the audit reveals:

        
Subject: I found 12 issues affecting your website's performance and SEO

Hi [Client Name],

I recently audited [Website] and found several issues that could be improving your traffic and conversion rate:

- Page load time is 6.2s (target: under 3s)
- Meta descriptions are missing on 8 pages
- Security headers are not configured

I can provide a detailed report and help you fix these issues. Would you be open to a 15-minute call?

Best,
[A.M. Rinas]
